Stats, Potions, and Training Limits enforces configurable caps on attributes, skills, potion consumption, and training sessions per level in OpenMW. Potions can be tracked in Counter mode (shared cooldown timer) or in an Oblivion-like Slots mode where each drink occupies an individual slot that frees up when its effect expires.
